N° 04 — The House

A Philosophy of Restraint

Est. MMXVIII
Seasons are a rhythm set by other people. We cut for the woman, not the calendar. Each piece is designed to outlive the trend that would have consumed it — a wardrobe inheritance, not a purchase.
A single Adélé cape takes eleven days. We do not rush the shoulder, we do not rush the hand-rolled hem, and we do not rush you. Slowness is the only honest way to make something that lasts.
Our atelier sits in Benin City, Edo State — where the craft of adornment is older than any fashion capital. Every piece leaves this room carrying that lineage, and arrives wherever you are.
One thread of gold runs through every garment we make — sometimes visible, sometimes hidden against the skin. It is the only signature we allow ourselves.
